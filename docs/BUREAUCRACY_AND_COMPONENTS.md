# KRED Bureaucracy, Components & Victory Reference

## 1. Components Reference

### 1.1 Pieces
- **Mark**: Lowest rank. Can be Removed. Promotes to Heel.
- **Heel**: Mid rank. Cannot be Removed by opponents. Promotes to Pawn.
- **Pawn**: Highest rank. Max 1 per player. Cannot be Removed or Influenced across Domains.

Counts by mode:
- 3P: 12 Marks, 9 Heels, 3 Pawns.
- 4P: 15 Marks, 13 Heels, 4 Pawns.
- 5P: 18 Marks, 17 Heels, 5 Pawns.

### 1.2 Tiles (24 Standard + 1 Blank)
Sum of all tile funding values = 100.
Face-down Bank tiles count toward Bureaucracy funding; face-up tiles do not.

---

## 2. Bureaucracy Phase

### Funding Calculation
Players sum funding values of face-down tiles in Bank.

### Turn Order
Highest funding total acts first. Ties broken by: Pawn in domain -> Most Heels -> Most Marks -> Most Credibility.

### Bureaucracy Action Pricing

| Cost (3P/4P) | Cost (5P) | Action |
|:---:|:---:|:---|
| 18 | 12 | Promote piece in **Office** (Mark -> Heel, Heel -> Pawn) |
| 15 | 10 | Take 1 Assist, Remove, or Influence action |
| 12 | 8 | Promote piece at **Rostrum** (Mark -> Heel, Heel -> Pawn) |
| 9 | 6 | Take 1 Advance, Withdraw, or Organize action |
| 6 | 4 | Promote piece in **Seat** (Mark -> Heel) |
| 3 | 2 | Restore 1 credibility notch |

---

## 3. Victory Conditions

A player wins when their Domain contains simultaneously:
- A piece (Mark or Heel) in **all 6 Seats**
- A **Heel** in **each Rostrum**
- A **Pawn** in the **Office**

Checked after Campaign move resolution and after Bureaucracy phase. Blank tile plays cannot trigger immediate win in 5P mode.
