### Task 3 note (PlayedTileState name clash)
Two `PlayedTileState` shapes coexist in the repo: the hook's (with `originalPieces`/`originalBoardTiles`) and the canonical `src/types/played-tile.ts` version (with `playedAt`/`gameStateSnapshot`). `CampaignProvider` uses the hook's shape — aligns with runtime usage in App.tsx. One cast in App.tsx line 866 (`playedTile: playedTile as any`) was required to bridge the two shapes when handing state to `pieceMovementHandlers` (which imports the canonical type). This mismatch was latent pre-refactor; making the hook's interface `export`ed surfaced it. Candidate for Step 8 cleanup.
